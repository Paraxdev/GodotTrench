# Model credits

`res://models` is the editor's Models panel root (see `crates/gt_editor/src/models.rs`). Models placed
here that were not made for this project keep their own license and credit, listed below by pack.
Machine readable versions live next to each pack as `pack.json`.

## Poly Haven (`godot/models/polyhaven`)
