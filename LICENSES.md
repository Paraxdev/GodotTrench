## Rust crates

Crates compiled into or used to build the editor ("editor"), or only used by its tests ("tests"), across all platform targets. Most are
MIT or Apache-2.0 licensed. Worth knowing:

* `imbl` and `imbl-sized-chunks` are MPL-2.0. They are used unmodified from crates.io, where their source stays
  available, which is all MPL-2.0 asks of a project that only links them.
* Where a crate offers a choice (for example `self_cell`: Apache-2.0 OR GPL-2.0-only, `r-efi`: MIT OR Apache-2.0 OR
  LGPL-2.1-or-later), GodotTrench uses it under the permissive option.

The table is generated by `python tools/gen_licenses.py`; rerun it after changing dependencies.
